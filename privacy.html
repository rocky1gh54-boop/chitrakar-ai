Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE DAALEIN]

Chitrakar AI ("hum", "hamari website") aapki privacy ka samman karta hai. Ye policy batati hai ki jab aap hamari website use karte hain to kya jaankari collect hoti hai aur use kaise kiya jaata hai.

Hum kya collect karte hain

  • Aapke likhe hue prompts, sirf image generate karne ke liye — hum inhe apne paas store nahi karte.
  • Standard analytics data jaise browser type, device, aur website par bitaya gaya samay, taaki website behtar banayi ja sake.
  • Cookies, jo advertising aur analytics services (jaise Google AdSense) dwara set ki ja sakti hain.

Google AdSense aur Cookies

Ye website Google AdSense use karti hai, jo third-party vendor hai. Google cookies ka use karta hai taaki aapko relevant ads dikha sake, jo aapke pehle ke internet visits par based hote hain. Aap Google Ad Settings mein jaakar personalized advertising ko band kar sakte hain: google.com/settings/ads

Third-party services

Image generation ke liye hum ek third-party AI API use karte hain. Jo prompt aap likhte hain wo generation ke liye us service ko bheja jaata hai, lekin hum khud koi personal identification data attach nahi karte.

Aapke rights

Aap kabhi bhi humse contact karke apne baare mein collected data ke baare mein pooch sakte hain ya usse delete karne ki request kar sakte hain.

Policy mein badlaav

Hum is policy ko samay-samay par update kar sakte hain. Koi bhi badlaav is page par turant reflect ho jaayega.

Contact
