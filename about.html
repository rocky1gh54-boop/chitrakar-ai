Chitrakar AI ke baare mein

Chitrakar AI ek muft tool hai jo shabdon ko images mein badalta hai. Iska maqsad simple hai — har kisi ko, bina kisi cost ya technical knowledge ke, apni kalpana ko visual roop dene ka mauka dena.

Ye kis liye banaya gaya

Chahe aap ek student ho jisse project ke liye illustration chahiye, ek content creator ho jisse social media ke liye images chahiye, ya bas curiosity se try karna chahte ho — Chitrakar AI sabke liye khula hai, bina login ya signup ke.

Ye kaise kaam karta hai

Jab aap koi prompt likhte hain, hamara system usse ek AI image-generation model ko bhejta hai jo text ko samajhkar ek naya, unique image banata hai. Har image apne aap generate hoti hai — koi bhi image kisi database se copy nahi ki jaati.

Hamara vaada

Ye tool hamesha free rahega. Website par kuch ads dikh sakte hain jo isse chalane mein madad karte hain, lekin core generator feature kisi ke liye bhi paid nahi hoga.
